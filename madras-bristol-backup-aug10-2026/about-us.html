About Us

Welcome to Madras

At Madras, we are more than just a place to dine; we are a culinary experience. Nestled in the heart of Patchway, Bristol, our restaurant is a testament to our . passion for food, hospitality, and creating memorable moments.

Welcome

Our Story

Madras – The food junction, where flavours of marina meet Bristol. Founded by a team of food enthusiasts, Madras was born from a shared love for flavours, a commitment to quality, and a desire to bring the flavours of authentic south Indian cuisine to the dining scene in Patchway, Bristol.
With years of culinary expertise, our chefs have meticulously crafted a menu that celebrates the Indian cuisine, offering a diverse range of options to suit every palate.

Our Philosophy

We believe that a great meal is more than just food; it's an amalgamation of art, culture, and togetherness. That's why every dish that leaves our kitchen is a masterpiece, made with the finest, locally-sourced ingredients. We prioritize sustainability and freshness, ensuring that your dining experience is not only delicious but also environmentally responsible.

Our Commitment

At Madras, we're not just in the business of serving food; we're in the business of creating memories. Our attentive and friendly staff is dedicated to providing you with impeccable service, making your visit a truly delightful one. Whether you're joining us for an intimate dinner, a celebratory event, or a casual lunch, we aim to exceed your expectations every time.

Community and Beyond

We're not just about food; we're about community. We take pride in being an active part of the local community, and we are committed to giving back. We support local charities, source ingredients from nearby farms, and create a warm and welcoming environment for all.
